Vortex Finance Module
=====================

1. Financial Ratios
- Current Ratio = Current Assets / Current Liabilities (>1.5 healthy)
- Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities (>1.0)
- Debt-to-Equity = Total Liabilities / Shareholder Equity (<2.0)
- ROI = (Gain - Cost) / Cost * 100
- ROE = Net Income / Shareholder Equity
- EPS = Net Income / Outstanding Shares

2. Time Value of Money
- PV = FV / (1 + r)^n
- FV = PV * (1 + r)^n
- NPV = sum of PV of all cash flows
- IRR: rate where NPV = 0
- Perpetuity: PV = PMT / r
- Annuity: PV = PMT * (1 - (1+r)^-n) / r

3. Risk Management
- Alpha: excess return vs benchmark
- Beta: volatility relative to market (>1 aggressive, <1 defensive)
- Sharpe Ratio: (Return - RiskFree) / Standard Deviation
- Value at Risk (VaR): max loss at 95/99% confidence
- Diversification: reduce unsystematic risk through uncorrelated assets

4. Tax Categories
- Income Tax: progressive brackets (10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%)
- Capital Gains: short-term (<1 year) as income, long-term (0%, 15%, 20%)
- Corporate Tax: flat 21% (US), varies by jurisdiction
- VAT/Sales Tax: consumption tax, varies by state/country (5-27%)
